# 30 short posts

For WhatsApp Channel, Telegram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram captions, X and TikTok captions. Written 2 November 2026. No statistics and no promises about results. Post one a day, add a guide link now and then, and change the words a little each time so it sounds like you.

## Tips

1. A CV is not a life story. It is a short advertisement for you. Keep it to two pages.
2. Start every line under a job with a verb: "Prepared", "Handled", "Trained". It reads faster.
3. Use an email address with your name in it. Nothing looks more professional.
4. Read your CV aloud before you send it. Your ears catch mistakes your eyes miss.
5. Change the first three lines of your CV for each job. Use the job title from the advert.
6. If you were out of work, studying or caring for family, say so in one honest line. A silent gap makes people guess.
7. Put your newest job first. Recruiters care most about what you did recently.
8. Do not write "hardworking team player" and stop. Show it with something you really did.
9. If it is not true, it does not belong on your CV. Interviewers ask about every line.
10. Save your CV as a PDF with real text, not a photo of a page. Software and phones read it better.

## Cover letters

11. Your cover letter should name the job. Copy the exact title from the advert.
12. "Dear Sir/Madam" goes with "Yours faithfully". "Dear Mr Mensah" goes with "Yours sincerely". Match them.
13. A cover letter is one page and three short paragraphs. Say the job, say why you fit, say thank you.
14. Never send the same cover letter to two employers without changing the name and the job.
15. A sample letter in the formal Ghanaian layout, free to copy and adapt: https://www.remarkablecv.com/guides/application-letter-sample-ghana

## Graduates and national service

16. National service is work. List it on your CV with your role, the place and your dates.
17. Fresh graduates: put your education near the top, then your service, then your projects.
18. No experience? You still have projects, a dissertation, leadership and skills. Use them.
19. Do not copy a CV from a friend. Employers read many, and they notice.
20. A short true line beats a long inflated one: https://www.remarkablecv.com/guides/nss-national-service-cv
